COP31 collapses: no deal as fossil fuels prove unmentionable

After a record 44 hours of overtime, the Antalya talks end on Sunday with no agreement — the first COP to close without a deal since 2000. The EU, UK, Panama and island states reject a final text that drops every mention of fossil fuels; Saudi Arabia and Russia had refused any wording at all. Unresolved items carry over to COP32 in Addis Ababa in 2027.
